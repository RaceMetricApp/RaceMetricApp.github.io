Privacy Policy

RaceMetric is a local-first karting, race-weekend, fitness, and driver-performance tracking app by JH43 Racing.

Summary

RaceMetric is designed to work without an account. The app stores your driver, session, race-weekend, logger, telemetry, session-note media, onboard video, fitness, circuit, and settings data locally on your device.

RaceMetric does not currently use third-party advertising, third-party analytics, cross-app tracking, or a developer-operated cloud account system.

RaceMetric may download read-only public category track-record reference data from RaceMetric's public GitHub data feed. This keeps track profiles accurate without requiring an app update, and it does not upload your RaceMetric data.

When event weather is enabled, RaceMetric sends the selected circuit coordinates and event date to Open-Meteo to request a forecast. It does not send your name, session history, fitness data, or notes.

Information Stored In The App

RaceMetric may store information you enter or choose inside the app, including:

  • Driver profile details, display preferences, dashboard logo, and driver image.
  • Kart sessions, lap and sector times, logger imports, telemetry, GPS traces, setup notes, kart class, tyres, pressures, gearing, engine details, conditions, and race results.
  • Race weekends, planned sessions, reminders, notes, and checklist items.
  • Circuit details, custom circuit locations, track notes, and circuit history.
  • Simulator sessions.
  • Workouts, weight entries, reaction-time history, step goal, and daily step display.
  • Friend or rival names and comparison lap times when you choose to record them.
  • Session notebook entries and photos you choose to attach.
  • Onboard footage you choose to attach, plus the local synchronization point used to align it with logger data.
  • Engine runtime, rebuild targets, maintenance-alert state, local notification read state, and reminder settings.
  • Short-lived cached circuit weather forecasts.
  • Coach videos or media you choose from your photo library.
  • Full backup JSON files when you choose to export them.

This information is stored locally on your device unless you choose to share or export it.

Permissions

Location

RaceMetric may ask for foreground location permission to help save circuit/session location context or support nearby circuit features. When event weather is enabled, coordinates for the selected circuit may be sent to Open-Meteo to provide its forecast. RaceMetric does not use background location tracking.

Motion And Fitness

RaceMetric may ask for Motion & Fitness permission to show your daily step count on the dashboard. Steps are used only for your own in-app activity view and do not affect the driver rating.

Photo Library And Videos

RaceMetric may ask for photo-library access so you can choose a dashboard logo, driver image, coach video, session-note photo, or onboard session footage. RaceMetric uses the media item you select and does not scan your full library. Selected session photos and videos may be copied into RaceMetric's local app storage so they remain attached to the saved session.

Files

RaceMetric may open a document picker so you can import supported logger files, such as native UniPro .uni sessions and UniPro TSV/CSV exports. The app reads the file you select so it can preview and store available laps, sectors, GPS, speed, RPM and sensor channels, then create or update a RaceMetric session. A retained local copy may be used for later session comparison and analysis.

Notifications

RaceMetric may ask for notification permission so it can schedule local reminders for race weekends and planned sessions, and one-time alerts for driver-set engine rebuild targets. These reminders and alerts are for app functionality, not advertising.

Onboard Video And Session Media

If you attach onboard footage, RaceMetric stores the selected video locally and saves the synchronization point you choose. This lets the app display lap time, speed, RPM, delta and track position alongside the video. RaceMetric does not upload the footage to a developer-operated server.

Session-note photos and coach reference videos are also stored locally. You can remove attached media from the relevant session or coaching entry inside the app.

Sharing And Export

RaceMetric includes share/export features for summaries and local backups. When you use these features, RaceMetric opens your device share sheet so you can choose where the information goes.

Only share or export information to people and services you trust.

Public Reference Data

RaceMetric may connect to a public GitHub-hosted JSON file to refresh category track-record reference data shown in the Tracks area. This request is for read-only app content. RaceMetric does not add your session, logger, location, fitness, photo, profile, or settings data to this request.

Weather Forecasts

Event weather is optional and can be turned off in Setup. When enabled, RaceMetric requests forecast data from Open-Meteo using the selected circuit coordinates and event date. Forecast responses are cached on the device for a short period and may be included in local race-weekend reminders.

Open-Meteo receives the internet request, which may include network information such as an IP address. Its handling of that request is governed by Open-Meteo's own privacy and service terms.

Data Collection And Tracking

RaceMetric does not send your saved RaceMetric records to a developer-operated server.

RaceMetric does not sell personal data.

RaceMetric does not use third-party advertising SDKs.

RaceMetric does not use third-party analytics SDKs.

RaceMetric does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Data Deletion

You can edit or delete many entries inside RaceMetric. Removing the app from your device may also remove locally stored RaceMetric data, imported logger files, session photos, and attached videos. If you have exported a backup or shared data outside the app, you are responsible for managing those copies.

Children And Young Drivers

RaceMetric may be used by young karting drivers. A parent or guardian should supervise use of the app, especially when sharing summaries, exporting backups, choosing media, or contacting support.

Changes To This Policy

This policy may be updated as RaceMetric changes. If future versions add accounts, cloud sync, analytics, or other data collection, this policy and the App Store privacy answers must be updated before release.

Contact
